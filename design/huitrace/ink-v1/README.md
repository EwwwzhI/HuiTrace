# 会声成迹 · 水墨图标提案

两道相向墨笔代表交谈，中央声纹代表录音，朱砂印点代表记录留痕。暖白底保证深浅任务栏上的主体对比。

使用内置 image_gen 生成。huitrace-ink.png 为真实 RGBA 原始图；huitrace-ink.ico 包含 16、20、24、32、40、48、64、96、128、256 像素。export.ps1 仅做尺寸和格式转换，可复现导出。

本目录为独立设计提案，没有替换应用图标。外缘仍有少量生成杂点；两次清边尝试生成了不透明棋盘格背景，已弃用。小尺寸由原图缩放，尚未手工做像素级简化。任务栏实际清晰度需要正式接入、构建后检查，不能仅凭 ICO 尺寸保证。

## 原始生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE exceptionally refined Windows desktop application icon for HuiTrace 会迹, a private meeting recorder that turns conversation into traceable notes. Design concept: 会声成迹, conversation leaves an ink trace. Chinese ink painting sensibility distilled into a bold iconic mark, not a landscape illustration. A warm ivory porcelain / xuan-paper rounded-square tile, seen perfectly straight on, fills 94% of a square canvas; outside its rounded corners is genuinely transparent alpha. Center a memorable emblem of TWO interlocking, facing, broad expressive near-black ink brush strokes suggesting two conversation bubbles meeting, with an elegant negative-space soundwave / flowing path between them. The mark must have a strong simple silhouette and open generous counters that remain intelligible at 24px. The strokes have restrained authentic ink density transitions and a few carefully controlled dry-brush edges; their core stays dense and crisp. A small vermilion square seal accent at lower right of the emblem provides a signature trace, no letters inside it. Palette: ivory, ink-black with subtle blue-green undertone, vermilion. Exquisite craftsmanship: subtly tactile ivory tile, fine rim highlight, very restrained dimensionality, polished edges, no thick bevel or chrome. Composition bold, emblem occupies 70% of tile, ample but not excessive margins. One isolated finished production icon only, no typography, no title, no presentation layout, no extra swatches, no mockup, no checkerboard, no drop shadow outside the tile. No generic microphone, no Latin H, no tiny detailed mountain scene. Square 1024x1024.
